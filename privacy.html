Privacy Policy & Disclaimer

Effective: November 20, 2026 · Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains how WageMaster Pro and 給与手取り計算Pro (“the Site”, “we”) handle information when you use the Site.

1. Calculator inputs stay in your browser

Salary, hourly rate, filing status and other values you enter into our calculators are processed entirely in your browser. They are never sent to or stored on our servers.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact form: if you contact us, we receive the name (a nickname is fine), email address and message you submit. Our contact form is provided by Google Forms (Google LLC), so your submission is stored on Google’s servers. We use it only to reply to you and to improve the Site.
  • Usage data: analytics and advertising services may collect information such as pages visited, browser type, device, approximate location and referring site, using cookies or similar technologies (see below).

We do not sell your personal information for money. We do not share contact-form information with third parties except where required by law.

3. Cookies

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. The Site and its partners may use them for analytics and advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some features may then not work as expected.

4. Analytics

We use these analytics tools to understand how the Site is used and to improve it:

  • Google Analytics (Google LLC) uses cookies to collect traffic data. When someone clicks a sponsored link below a calculator result, we record which slot was clicked and the period shown (year, month and so on), but never the amounts entered. You can opt out with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics (Cloudflare, Inc.) counts page views and measures page speed without cookies or local storage.

The Site may use Google AdSense, a third-party advertising service. If it does:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.

For details, see How Google uses cookies in advertising. If we show personalized ads to visitors in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we will ask for consent where the law requires it.

6. Affiliate disclosure

The Site may include affiliate links. Affiliate links are labeled “sponsored”. If you click one and make a purchase or sign up, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate relationships do not change how our calculators work.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example California and other U.S. states, or the EEA/UK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, and to opt out of targeted advertising. Under some U.S. state laws, advertising cookies may be considered a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information; you can opt out using the Google Ads Settings and aboutads.info links above, or by enabling Global Privacy Control in your browser. To make a request, use our contact form.
