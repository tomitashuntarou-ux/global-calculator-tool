    How this salary to hourly calculator works

    Employers quote pay in different ways: a yearly salary, a monthly figure, or an hourly rate. This calculator puts them on the same scale so you can compare job offers, check a raise, or price your time as a freelancer. Enter your pay, your weekly hours and the weeks you are paid, and every figure updates instantly.

    The take-home estimate subtracts 401(k) savings, federal income tax after the standard deduction, Social Security, Medicare and the state tax rate you enter. Gross pay is what you earn; net pay is what lands in your bank account.

    Salary to hourly formula

    Hourly wage = annual salary ÷ (hours per week × weeks per year). For a full-time job that is 2,080 hours a year. To go the other way, multiply your hourly rate by the same 2,080 hours.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I convert salary to hourly pay?

    Divide your annual salary by the hours you work in a year. For a standard full-time job that is 40 hours x 52 weeks = 2,080 hours, so a $52,000 salary equals $25 per hour.

    How many working hours are in a year?

    The standard figure is 2,080 hours (40 hours x 52 weeks). If you take two weeks unpaid, it drops to 2,000 hours.

    How accurate is the take-home pay estimate?

    It is an estimate. It uses 2026 federal tax brackets, the standard deduction, Social Security and Medicare, plus the flat state rate you enter. Local taxes, health insurance, credits and other deductions are not included.

    What is the difference between gross and net pay?

    Gross pay is what you earn before anything is taken out. Net pay, or take-home pay, is what reaches your bank account after taxes and deductions.

    Which US states have no income tax on wages?

    Alaska, Florida, Nevada, New Hampshire, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Washington and Wyoming do not tax wages. Enter 0 as your state tax rate if you live in one of them.
