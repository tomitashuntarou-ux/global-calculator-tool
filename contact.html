What WageMaster Pro does

WageMaster Pro is a free salary-to-hourly converter and take-home pay calculator for US workers. Use it to compare job offers, check what a raise or overtime is really worth after tax, and see how 401(k) contributions change your paycheck.

Everything you type is calculated in your browser. Nothing is sent to our servers.

Sources and how we keep figures current

Tax figures are based on official publications, mainly:

  • Internal Revenue Service (IRS): federal brackets and standard deduction (Rev. Proc. 2025-32 for tax year 2026)
  • Social Security Administration: Social Security wage base
  • State revenue departments: state income tax rates

We review the calculators when new annual figures are published and update them as soon as we can confirm the changes. Results are estimates; see our Privacy Policy & Disclaimer.

Ads and affiliate links

To keep the tool free, we may show ads and affiliate links. Affiliate links are labeled “sponsored”, and we may earn a commission if you sign up through one, at no extra cost to you. We choose products that fit the next step after a calculation, such as filing taxes or saving the money you keep.
